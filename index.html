About BELLA~B

Denver Roots, Austin Expansion

BELLA~B moves like she’s already booked — because she is. What started in Denver’s late-night circuit turned into a steady climb built on packed rooms, repeat sets, and a reputation for knowing exactly what the floor needs (sometimes before it does).


Now splitting time between Denver and Austin, she’s expanding outward — tighter sets, heavier pressure, bigger rooms. No overthinking, no filler, no “one more track” that kills the energy. Just movement, connection, and a sound that keeps people locked in longer than they planned.
